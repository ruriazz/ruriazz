## About me

- 💣 I'm **Aziz Ruri Suparman** — a self-taught **Pull Stack Developer** based in **Jakarta, Indonesia** (UTC+07:00). I just pull things off the Internet and put them into my code.
- 🤖 Backend by trade, builder by habit — I work across **Go, Python, TypeScript, PHP, and Java**, and lately I build around **AI agent orchestration**, **MCP servers**, and **open-source developer tooling**.
- 🧩 I enjoy turning complex problems into simple ones, and I'm always learning new things.

## What I do

[Backend-focused software engineer in Jakarta](https://ruriazz.com) with **5+ years** building scalable systems for **fintech and marketplace** platforms.

- **Software Engineer (Full Stack) · PT Bank SMBC Indonesia** — 2025–Present, South Jakarta. Stack: Spring Boot, NestJS, Next.js, WebRTC, WebSocket, Redis. Built a **WebRTC video-call platform** for internal banking divisions and **Jenius** customers, with an auto-assignment call-routing engine and a **Redis Pub/Sub** realtime queue (hundreds of video calls a month).
- **Backend Software Engineer · PT Oto Klix Indonesia (Otoklix)** — 2022–2024. Led a **MySQL → Elasticsearch migration** that cut search latency from ~10s to **under 2s (~5x)** in 4 months, plus geo-aware search.
- **Web Programmer · PT Togu Inovasi Teknologi** — 2020–2021. First to ship a mobile app and pioneered **WebRTC** ("Wello").
